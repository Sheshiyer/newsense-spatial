# Newsense Portfolio Signal Map

Date: `2026-05-13`

## Purpose

Map the recovered Newsense portfolio into the refined storytelling framework so
the project atlas is grounded in actual work and not abstract object language
alone.

## Portfolio Thesis

The existing Newsense portfolio already shows a pattern:

- products and launches made legible through motion
- campaigns made memorable through conceptual systems
- spaces and objects made resonant through editorial restraint

This is the practical proof of the Newsense story:

- complexity shaped into signal

## Canonical Story Families

## Family A: Signal Construction

Equivalent taxonomy:

- `campaign-system`

Meaning:

- Newsense takes a vague or overloaded brief and gives it a memorable structure

Primary object language:

- slabs
- rings
- layered assemblies

Projects:

- `re-outland`
- `oneplus-softgold`
- `oneplus-lava-red`
- `kaa`
- `agm-x2-football`

## Family B: Signal Release

Equivalent taxonomy:

- `motion`

Meaning:

- Newsense gives movement, pace, energy, and clarity to a product or campaign

Primary object language:

- discs
- cylinders
- directional clusters

Projects:

- `ikodoo`
- `agm-glory`
- `agm-x2`
- `agm-x3`
- `agm-x5`
- `op-roadtrip`

## Family C: Signal Framing

Equivalent taxonomy:

- `editorial-stills`

Meaning:

- Newsense reveals atmosphere, material, and mood through restraint and careful
  framing

Primary object language:

- framed planes
- quiet light-boxes
- shallow architectural surfaces

Projects:

- `oneplus-experience-store`
- `divine-heritage`

## Family D: Noise Shadow

Equivalent taxonomy:

- `archive-fragment`

Meaning:

- material that is preserved for provenance but not promoted as active signal

Primary object language:

- lower-contrast blocks
- suppressed shelf pieces

Projects:

- `alpha-source`
- `arte-artifacts`
- `cider-and-milk`

## Studio Story Consequence

The `Studio` route should not describe Newsense as a generic service bundle.

1. identify the signal
2. shape the system
3. release it through motion, image, or space
